Real boats: does the smart search still win?

Same searches, but the boats are real vessels from NOAA ship-tracking (AIS) data for Jan 1, 2023 in US waters: a random boat, a random moment it was moving, then where it actually went. Real boats slow down, turn, and stop. A smart search that assumes straight lines loses most of its lead; one that learns from real tracks on other days (Jan 2–3) gets much of it back.

These boats had their trackers on and weren't running from anyone. 2,000 test boats per row, drawn from about 200–250 different vessels, so the real uncertainty is bigger than a single run suggests. One test day only.

What the simulation assumes

These are assumptions, not field data. The two that matter most: how far a drone's camera can really spot a small boat at sea, and whether boats hold a straight course. The straight-line boats hold one course; the real-boat options replay recorded fishing-boat paths, which turn, wander, and stop.
